Privacy policy

Updated 4 October 2026 · Add-in Downloader 1.0 · Developer: HANWEI LI

No developer-side collection

The app contains no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking or cloud account synchronization. It does not upload your school account, course content, filenames, browsing history, cookies or tokens to the developer. Course requests go directly to your chosen learning platform. Emails you choose to send for support are received by the developer; do not include credentials or private course content.

Safari extension

The extension reads supported course pages on websites you authorize to identify resources, sections and file types. Scans and preparation make authenticated requests through Safari’s existing session. The extension does not read or export cookie values or store school passwords. Candidate files and selections are kept in page memory. A same-tab, same-site session flag supports reopening the panel during navigation. Closing the panel stops reopening and cancels pending scan/preparation requests, but does not cancel browser downloads already started.

Optional Mac app connection

The optional native Moodle feature uses its own web session or a personal token stored in macOS Keychain. It does not import Safari cookies. Course selections, baselines, file fingerprints and download records are saved locally to detect new or updated materials. App-owned web session cookies may be used by native requests; they are not exported to the developer. Checks run when you click, not on a background schedule.

Files and permissions

Safari downloads follow Safari’s settings. Separately, the sandboxed Mac app uses Downloads read/write permission to save files from native checks and the local example, handle duplicate names safely, and reveal successful saves in Finder. It does not delete downloaded files. The Safari extension does not carry this Downloads entitlement.

Website access is granted through Safari; grant only the sites you need. Shared app-group preferences store the selected UI language and the course information you explicitly send from the extension to the app. This sharing is local, not cloud synchronization.

Your controls

Change language in the app or panel. Disable the extension or revoke website access in Safari. Disconnect the native account to remove its login state, token and course index; already saved files remain. Remove a watched course or rebuild its baseline without deleting local downloads.

Other services

Your institution may record page access, file requests or viewed/completion states. Safari and macOS maintain their own browser, download and permission data. Public help and sample pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, subject to GitHub’s hosting policies; do not include private course data in requests to those pages. These services are not operated by this app’s developer.
