A Mac Safari extension · One-time purchase · No subscription

Choose your course files. Download them together.

Scan supported course content, filter by section or file type, select the materials you need and start downloads in Safari. Keep the server’s filenames and your Safari download location.

  • Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard and Brightspace standard course content structures.
  • Documents, presentations, spreadsheets, images, archives, text and directly downloadable audio/video.
  • Assignments, quizzes, discussions, submissions, external cloud links and streaming recordings are excluded.
  • Partial scans and inaccessible branches are reported, not presented as complete course lists.

Your language, across the app and extension

English, 简体中文, Español, 日本語, Deutsch, Français, 한국어, Português (Brasil), 繁體中文. Choose on first use or change later. Course titles, filenames and course content retain their original language.

Optional, on-demand checks for new Moodle files

The Mac app can connect to Moodle separately. Choose courses and establish a baseline without downloading historical materials. Later manual checks save supported new or updated materials to Downloads. No background monitoring. A local example lets you try the workflow without a school account.

Compatibility

macOS 14 or later. School themes, permissions, SSO policies and platform versions vary. Standard structures are supported; not every school has been tested. The public sample is original demonstration content, not a live school or official LMS service.
